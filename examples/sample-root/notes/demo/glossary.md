# Demo glossary

- **Artifact**: a page with a shape (a plan, a review, a guide). Its source is
  `<slug>/artifact.json`; `index.html` and `artifact.md` are built from it.
- **Note**: a short living fact, rewritten in place, under about 4 KB.
- **Hub**: the generated page for one project, at `/projects/<project>/`.
- **Anchor**: a block's stable id, such as `sec-feedback-loop.p1`. Feedback
  and comment threads point at anchors.
